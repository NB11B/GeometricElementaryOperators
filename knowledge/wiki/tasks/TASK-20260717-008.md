# TASK-20260717-008 — Add multi-prime discovery and witness reduction

## Objective

Extend the physically validated v1 exact-search engine into a discovery layer that can generate nearby candidate statements, search them over multiple finite fields, reduce exact witnesses, and extract canonical blade-wise polynomial differences for symbolic follow-up.

## V2 scope

- deterministic prime-matrix expansion of an identity corpus;
- automatic one-edit mutation generation;
- mutation provenance and stable mutation identifiers;
- exact CPU screening before CUDA execution;
- multi-prime result aggregation;
- witness minimization by coefficient magnitude and active blade support;
- canonical polynomial extraction for bounded-degree expressions;
- candidate ranking by cross-prime survival, expression size, and witness complexity;
- machine-readable discovery reports;
- a CUDA runner that preserves the v1 evidence contract.

## Acceptance gate

- v1 corpus remains unchanged and continues to pass;
- generated mutation corpus is byte-for-byte deterministic;
- each mutation records its source identity and edit operation;
- known false mutations are rejected over every configured prime;
- known identities survive each configured finite-field sample set;
- GPU witnesses reproduce exactly on the host;
- reduced witnesses remain valid;
- polynomial extraction agrees with exact evaluation on deterministic checks;
- result validator reports `VALIDATION: PASS`;
- evidence manifest and optional archive are created.

## Interpretation boundary

Cross-prime survival remains computational evidence, not a proof over characteristic zero. Polynomial normalization may enable subsequent proof, but only after the extracted identity is independently verified by a symbolic or formal system.
